## 🚀 Step-by-Step: Submit to Google Search Console

### Step 1: Access Google Search Console
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click **"Add Property"**

### Step 2: Verify Your Website
Choose one verification method:

**Option A: HTML File Upload (Easiest)**
1. Google will give you an HTML file to download
2. Upload it to your `/public/` folder
3. Click "Verify" in Search Console

**Option B: HTML Meta Tag**
1. Google gives you a meta tag like: `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="xxxxx" />`
2. I can add this to your index.html file
3. Click "Verify" in Search Console

### Step 3: Submit Your Sitemap
1. In Google Search Console, go to **"Sitemaps"** (left sidebar)
2. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click **"Submit"**
4. Wait for Google to process (can take a few hours to days)

### Step 4: Request Indexing (Important!)
1. In Search Console, go to **"URL Inspection"** (top bar)
2. Enter your homepage: `https://manuhimalayanadventure.in`
3. Click **"Request Indexing"**
4. Repeat for important pages like:
   - `https://manuhimalayanadventure.in/#/river-rafting`
   - `https://manuhimalayanadventure.in/#/paragliding`
   - `https://manuhimalayanadventure.in/#/packages`
